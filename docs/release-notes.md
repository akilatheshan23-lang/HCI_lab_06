# FitFlow 1.1.0 (2)

Find your rhythm with an all-new violet-and-mint interface.

- Create a private profile and choose your daily focus.
- Explore six guided, timed movement routines.
- Pause, resume, and record completed sessions.
- Keep a simple food and water journal with live totals.
- Follow seven-day movement and weight history.
- Save small wins in a clearly labeled local community demo.
- Edit, export, or delete your local data from your profile.

This release runs offline on Android 8.0+ and includes a responsive browser preview. Daily recommendations are rule-based. Real social sharing, cloud accounts, generative AI, Play distribution, and iOS/TestFlight are not included. Existing project baseline: no previous application source or release existed in the repository; version 1.1.0 (2) establishes the requested incremented release numbering, not a claim of a previously published version.
