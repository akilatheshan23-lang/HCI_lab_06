**App name:** FitFlow

**Short description:** Build your daily rhythm with workouts, food journals, and simple progress.

**Category:** Health & Fitness

**Full description:**

Make a little room for yourself with FitFlow. Choose a guided movement routine, keep a simple food and water journal, and look back on the progress you have recorded.

Six timed routines offer gentle mobility, low-impact cardio, and bodyweight strength sessions. Pause when you need to and record your completed movement. Your chosen focus helps Daily Flow suggest a routine using local rules.

Log meals with values from food packaging or a trusted source, record water, and choose your own journal targets. View recent workouts, a seven-day activity chart, and weight check-ins.

Your profile and journals stay on your device. No account is needed. You can edit, export, or delete your local records. The community screen is a private demonstration with sample posts and local notes; it does not publish to other users.

FitFlow is a student wellness project for adults, not a medical service. This Android release does not use generative AI, ads, analytics, or a cloud backend.
